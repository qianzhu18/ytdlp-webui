# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Muku a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Show empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus discussion on the project and its users
- Take responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes
- Respect privacy, credentials, copyrighted material, and platform rules

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, discrimination, or sexualized attention
- Trolling, deliberately disruptive discussion, or sustained off-topic attacks
- Publishing another person's private information or credentials without permission
- Submitting secrets, cookies, private content, or material without the right to share it
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy. They may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or serious violations.

Report conduct issues privately to `2729478828@qq.com`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential to the extent practical.
